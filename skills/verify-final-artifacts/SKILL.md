---
name: verify-final-artifacts
description: Inspect final exported documents, figures, or packaged visuals when fonts, labels, pagination, or source claims can differ from editable originals.
---

Identify the exact user-facing file and intended display, print, or export size. Use available format-specific rendering tools or skills for mechanics. Inspect the rendered deliverable after its last material edit; an earlier preview or successful export cannot certify a later file.

Check the affected visual boundaries: clipping, label collisions, missing or substituted glyphs, numeral integrity, legibility, pagination, and captions. For diagrams, confirm connectors and labels still express the intended relationships. For numeric figures, compare plotted values and units with their underlying data. Do not add unrelated visual redesign work.

Verify claims embedded in the artifact, including source attribution, captions, quotes, and edition labels against inspected evidence where applicable. Keep generated interpretation distinguishable from source material. A visually plausible label is not evidence of correct provenance.

After correcting defects, re-render and inspect the changed pages/regions and any affected pagination or layout. Use representative pages plus all changed or high-risk regions for larger artifacts; identify uninspected coverage rather than declaring every page reviewed.

Record the delivered path/version or hash and what was actually inspected. Keep editable source and export identities clear, use durable output locations, and link the real final deliverable. If the renderer or required viewing environment is unavailable, complete available checks and name the remaining visual boundary without claiming it passed.
